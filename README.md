## Description
<!--
Give a short description on what your project accomplishes and what tools is uses. Basically, what problems does it solve and why it's different from other apps in the app store.
-->
NeuroMallea is a comprehensive mental health and sleep companion application built on CareKit and ResearchKit. It provides users with tailored care plans including CBT exercises, sleep tracking, and panic attack logging. NeuroMallea differentiates itself by offering rich CareKit UI components (including custom Swift Charts), dynamic ResearchKit Active Tasks (Stroop, Range of Motion), and seamless cloud synchronization using ParseCareKit.

NeuroMallea is designed for individuals looking to actively manage their mental health, track symptoms of depression or anxiety, and improve their sleep hygiene. It is also highly suitable for patients participating in clinical studies that require daily symptom tracking.

## Contributions/Features
1. **Custom Tasks and Care Plans**: Added multiple Care Plans (Mental Health, Sleep Health, Stress Reduction) containing dynamic tasks such as Bedtime Checklists, Nausea/Panic Attack logging, and daily medication adherence.
2. **ResearchKit Integration**: Implemented a tailored onboarding flow (Consent and Welcome steps) and integrated multiple Active Tasks including the Stroop test, Range of Motion, and Guided Meditation. Also included a PHQ-9 Survey.
3. **Custom UI and Swift Charts**: Added an Insights tab featuring Custom Swift Charts to visualize user progress. Replaced standard CareKit views with CustomFeaturedContentViews.
4. **Profile and Contact Management**: Replaced the standard ContactView with a Searchable Contact View, and upgraded the ProfileView to utilize a Form with multiple contact inputs.

## Challenges faced while developing
<!--
Describe any challenges you faced with learning Swift, your baseline app, or adding features. You can describe how you overcame them.
-->
One of the main challenges was understanding and integrating ResearchKit's Active Tasks into CareKit's architecture. Additionally, embedding custom SwiftUI views (like Swift Charts) into CareKit's standard UIKit `OCKListViewController` required learning about `UIHostingController` and CareKit's generic `EventQueryView` wrappers.
